Proxacare Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

Proxacare is a family caregiving organizer app designed for personal use.

  • All data (profiles, notes, medications, and visits) is stored locally on your device.
  • No information is transmitted to any external server.
  • iCloud backup is optional and handled entirely by Apple.
  • Users must have permission to enter information about loved ones.
  • You can delete all data at any time by removing the app from your device.

For privacy questions, contact: support@icloud.com
