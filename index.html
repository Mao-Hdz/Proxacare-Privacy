Proxacare Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

Overview

Proxacare is a family caregiving organizer app designed for personal use. The app helps users manage caregiving information such as loved ones’ profiles, notes, medications, and visits — all stored locally on their device. Proxacare does not collect, share, or transmit any user data to external servers or third parties.

Data Storage and iCloud Backup

All information you enter in Proxacare is stored securely on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your device may include Proxacare’s local data in your personal iCloud backup. This process is managed entirely by Apple, and Proxacare does not have access to or control over your iCloud account or backup data.

Proxacare does not transmit, analyze, or store any information outside of your device. No servers, analytics tools, or third-party services are used by the app.

User Responsibility

  • All caregiving information is created and managed by you on your own device.
  • You must have permission to record information about loved ones.
  • You can delete all data at any time by removing the app from your device.
  • iCloud backup can be disabled at any time through your iPhone or iPad settings.

Data Collection

Proxacare does not collect or transmit any data. All data remains private and encrypted within your device and iCloud account. No user information is accessible to the Proxacare team, developers, or third parties.

Security

All information stored on your device is protected by Apple’s security features, including Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode, depending on your device settings. When backed up to iCloud, your data is encrypted by Apple’s infrastructure and remains private to your Apple ID.

Contact

For questions or concerns about privacy or data security, contact us at: support@proxacare.com
